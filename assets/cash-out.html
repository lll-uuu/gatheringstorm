Who speculates on our futures?

Brazil blocked betting sites before its 2026 presidential election, yet anyone with a crypto wallet could still bet on who would govern the country, from anywhere, without leaving a name. Cash Out followed that money on Polymarket during the runoff between Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro.

Every hour until polls closed on 25 October 2026, the work recorded every bet placed on the runoff and turned it into a marbled image, one drop of paint per bet. Red for Lula, yellow and green for Flávio, white for unusually large bets and newly created wallets.

Alongside the images runs an open investigation into whose money moved the odds, when, and where it came from. It uses only public data from Polymarket and the Polygon blockchain, and publishes figures about groups of wallets, never about individuals.
